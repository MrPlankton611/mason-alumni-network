Comet Career Speakers

Program where alumni volunteer to come back to MHS and share life and career perspective with current students. These engagements could be with a class, club, or special career discussion event with multiple students, typically set up by our Experiential Learning Program (XLP). They're usually informal and involve sharing your career journey, career description, and career success advice.

Who: Career alumni and current students
What to expect: No program-specific follow-up questions — just an email letting you know: “You are all set! One of our Program leaders will contact you about specific opportunities for you to choose from.”
Sign Up to Learn More

Comet Job Shadow/Career Discussion Program

Alumni provide job shadows or career discussion one-on-ones with MHS students, whether virtual or in-person. These engagements are typically with 1-2 current MHS students, set up by our Experiential Learning Program (XLP). They're usually informal, with students asking questions related to your career and job — and for in-person job shadows, getting to see your work environment firsthand.

Who: Career alumni and current students
What to expect: We'll email you asking whether you can offer an in-person job shadow, in-person career discussion, or virtual career discussion, and what dates/times work best.
Sign Up to Learn More

Internship Program

Alumni provide paid or unpaid internships for MHS students, giving them real-world experience and mentorship. Internships are set up in conjunction with our Experiential Learning Program (XLP) and can be in-person, hybrid, or remote.

Who: Career alumni and MHS students
What to expect: We'll email you asking for a brief description of the internship role/job, the timeframe/hours required, and required qualifications (age/skills).
